*sat-7*

Joss did it. My brother did it. Fine. There. Written down. Everyone already knew, it's not like I'm telling anyone anything, the record had it before I ever said it to myself so what does it even matter that I'm saying it now, except it does, I don't know why it does but it does, my hand actually feels different writing it than my head does thinking it, which is a stupid thing to notice and I'm noticing it anyway.

They took his access first. Then the name got stuck to it forever, not like paper, paper at least fades, this doesn't fade, it just sits there for anyone who thinks to look, and I looked, once, just to see, and it showed me everything, of course it did, that's the whole design of everything now isn't it, nothing hidden, nothing left to wonder about, except I've got about four hundred things I'm still wondering about and none of them are in any record anywhere.

Ice on the trough this morning. First time this season. Broke it with my hand instead of asking for the heater fix, which I could get in about four minutes if I wanted it, I don't know why I don't want it, my palm was stinging for an hour after and I kind of liked that, which probably says something I don't want to look at directly right now so I'm not going to.

He used to leave the water running when he brushed his teeth. Every time. I told him a hundred times, we don't need to save it, nobody's counting anymore, and he'd get this look, embarrassed almost, and do it anyway, and once he said something like *somebody should still act like it matters even if it doesn't* and I thought that was such a strange thing to say and didn't think about it again for years and now I think about it basically every morning with my hand in ice water for no actual reason.

*sun-8*

Found the heir's name in something he left. Not hidden, he never hid anything, that was almost the worst part of him honestly, just left everything sitting out like consequences happened to other people. A thread. Half a conversation. The other half gone. Something about a meeting, something about people who don't think the way everyone thinks now, utopia hackers, they actually call themselves that, with a straight face apparently, I can't tell if that's a joke or if they're completely serious and I've read it maybe nine times now trying to decide and I still can't tell.

I want to find them.

I don't actually know what for.

Spent the whole afternoon on the ridge checking snares that didn't need checking, nothing in them, wouldn't have mattered if there was, I could have meat at the door tonight without moving from this chair. Went anyway. Some kind of quiet up there my actual life doesn't have anymore. Not since. The system still finds me even out past where the road gives up, still asks twice a week if I need anything, delivers before I finish asking, I've never turned any of that off, I'm not pretending to live in the past, I just want a few things a day that cost me something, that's all, that's the whole reasoning, if you can call it reasoning.

*sun-15*

Camus. Some name I keep landing on. Old, dead, before any of this, before the Settling, before whatever made Joss's whole life possible and also apparently made it easy enough to throw away in about four seconds. Wrote something about rebellion I read four times because I didn't believe it the first three. *I rebel, therefore we exist.* Not therefore I'm right. Not therefore anything changes. Just the refusing itself, sitting there, being real, whether it goes anywhere or not, and I don't know if that's the most profound thing I've ever read or just a very nice way of admitting you don't have a reason, and I honestly think it's both, and I stood out past the treeline for a long time tonight thinking about that, cold enough to see my breath, no actual reason to be standing there, and then I wasn't thinking about Camus anymore at all, I was thinking about whether Joss ever stood outside anywhere like that, whether he had a treeline, whether he had anything at all like this, and I don't know why my brain went there, it just did, it does that now, goes places without telling me first.

*thu-19*

He came out here once, a few days, hardly ever did that, said the city was getting to him. Looked at the animals, the woodpile, the trough, and instead of the usual teasing he just went quiet and said he understood it better than he used to. Didn't ask what he meant. Should have asked what he meant. Looked completely normal the whole visit, nothing on his face at all, and I've gone over that so many times there's a groove in it now, either he was better at hiding things than I ever gave him credit for or there wasn't anything to hide yet and it happened later, all at once, the way you don't notice you're holding your breath until you've held it too long, and I don't know which one I want to be true and I don't think either one actually helps.

Read something else about him today. He'd tried to reach them himself, months before, before Otis, there's a gap right where that thread would answer the only thing I actually care about — whether he did it because of them or went looking for them because he'd already decided and wanted somewhere to put the decision so it would look like it meant something instead of just being what it was. I don't want to know which. I need to know which. Both true at once and I don't have anywhere to put that so I went and split more wood than I'll burn all season, more than I know what to do with honestly, there's a whole extra stack now sitting there for no reason, doing something with my arms instead of whatever my head's doing, which is apparently just this, apparently just circling the same four facts until they wear a hole through something.
